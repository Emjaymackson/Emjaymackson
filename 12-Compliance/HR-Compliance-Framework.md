# HR Compliance Framework

## 1. Purpose

This framework provides a structured approach to identifying, assessing, monitoring, and addressing HR compliance risks.

---

## 2. Compliance Areas

### Employment

Review:

* Employment contracts
* Job descriptions
* Employee records
* Terms and conditions

### Recruitment

Review:

* Recruitment documentation
* Candidate communications
* Selection processes
* Background checks where appropriate
* Equal treatment

### Employee Relations

Review:

* Disciplinary procedures
* Grievances
* Investigations
* Documentation
* Appeals

### Payroll and Benefits

Review:

* Salary records
* Benefits
* Statutory deductions
* Payroll approvals

### Data Protection

Review:

* Employee information
* Access controls
* Data retention
* Confidentiality

---

## 3. Compliance Review Process

```text
Identify Requirement
        ↓
Assess Current Practice
        ↓
Identify Gap
        ↓
Assess Risk
        ↓
Corrective Action
        ↓
Owner & Deadline
        ↓
Verification
        ↓
Ongoing Monitoring
```

---

## 4. Risk Classification

### Low

Limited impact and manageable through routine controls.

### Moderate

Requires defined corrective action and monitoring.

### High

Potentially significant legal, financial, operational, employee, or reputational consequences.

High-risk matters should be escalated appropriately.

---

## 5. Documentation

Maintain evidence of:

* Policies
* Approvals
* Employee acknowledgements
* Training
* Reviews
* Investigations
* Corrective actions
* Audit findings

---

## 6. HRBP Role

The HRBP should:

* Identify risk.
* Provide practical guidance.
* Coordinate corrective action.
* Track outstanding issues.
* Escalate serious concerns.
* Partner with legal and leadership where necessary.

---

## 7. Governance

Each compliance area should have:

* Owner
* Requirement
* Control
* Review frequency
* Evidence
* Risk rating
* Action plan
