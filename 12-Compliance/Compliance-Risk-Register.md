# Compliance Risk Register

## Purpose

A compliance risk register provides a central record of identified HR compliance risks and the actions required to manage them.

---

## Risk Register

| Risk                              | Area          | Impact | Likelihood | Rating   | Owner | Action           |
| --------------------------------- | ------------- | ------ | ---------- | -------- | ----- | ---------------- |
| Incomplete employee records       | HR Operations | High   | Medium     | High     | HR    | Records audit    |
| Outdated policy                   | Governance    | Medium | Medium     | Moderate | HRBP  | Policy review    |
| Inconsistent disciplinary process | ER            | High   | Medium     | High     | HR    | Manager training |
| Poor data access control          | Data          | High   | Low        | Moderate | HR/IT | Access review    |
| Incomplete onboarding             | Recruitment   | Medium | Medium     | Moderate | HR    | Checklist        |

The table is illustrative.

---

## Risk Assessment

### Impact

Consider:

* Legal exposure
* Financial impact
* Operational disruption
* Employee impact
* Reputational consequences

### Likelihood

Consider:

* Frequency
* Existing controls
* Historical incidents
* Process weaknesses

---

## Risk Response

Possible responses:

* Reduce
* Control
* Monitor
* Escalate
* Transfer where appropriate
* Accept within approved risk tolerance

---

## Action Tracking

Each high-priority risk should have:

* Action owner
* Deadline
* Required resources
* Status
* Evidence
* Review date

---

## HRBP Use

The HRBP should regularly review whether:

* Risks are increasing.
* Controls remain effective.
* Actions are overdue.
* Similar issues are recurring.
* Leadership escalation is required.

---

## Governance

The register should be reviewed periodically and updated whenever significant changes occur.
