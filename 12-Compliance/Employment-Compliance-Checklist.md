# Employment Compliance Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist provides a high-level framework for reviewing common HR compliance areas.

It should be adapted to the organization's jurisdiction, industry, workforce structure, and legal requirements.

---

## 1. Employment Documentation

* [ ] Employment contracts are issued appropriately.
* [ ] Job descriptions are available.
* [ ] Employee records are maintained.
* [ ] Employment terms are documented.
* [ ] Changes to employment terms are recorded.

---

## 2. Recruitment

* [ ] Roles have defined requirements.
* [ ] Recruitment processes are documented.
* [ ] Selection criteria are consistent.
* [ ] Candidate information is handled appropriately.
* [ ] Recruitment decisions are documented where necessary.

---

## 3. Onboarding

* [ ] New-hire documentation completed.
* [ ] Employee records created.
* [ ] Required policies communicated.
* [ ] Role expectations explained.
* [ ] Required training completed.

---

## 4. Attendance and Leave

* [ ] Attendance records maintained.
* [ ] Leave processes documented.
* [ ] Leave approvals recorded.
* [ ] Absence issues addressed consistently.
* [ ] Relevant statutory requirements reviewed.

---

## 5. Performance Management

* [ ] Performance expectations documented.
* [ ] Reviews conducted consistently.
* [ ] Performance concerns documented.
* [ ] Improvement support provided.
* [ ] Decisions appropriately recorded.

---

## 6. Employee Relations

* [ ] Grievance process available.
* [ ] Disciplinary process documented.
* [ ] Investigations handled appropriately.
* [ ] Employee responses considered.
* [ ] Appeals process available where applicable.

---

## 7. Compensation

* [ ] Salary records maintained.
* [ ] Payroll approvals documented.
* [ ] Benefits recorded.
* [ ] Statutory obligations reviewed.
* [ ] Compensation changes approved.

---

## 8. Data Protection

* [ ] Employee data access is controlled.
* [ ] Sensitive information is appropriately protected.
* [ ] Records are retained appropriately.
* [ ] Data sharing follows approved processes.
* [ ] Employee information is not unnecessarily disclosed.

---

## 9. Health and Safety

* [ ] Workplace risks identified.
* [ ] Relevant procedures communicated.
* [ ] Incidents recorded.
* [ ] Employees receive required safety information.

---

## 10. Policy Governance

* [ ] Policies have owners.
* [ ] Policies have approval dates.
* [ ] Policies are communicated.
* [ ] Policies are reviewed periodically.
* [ ] Policy changes are documented.

---

## 11. Audit Evidence

Maintain appropriate evidence for completed controls.

Examples:

* Signed documents
* System records
* Approval records
* Training records
* Meeting records
* Audit reports
* Corrective action records

---

## Important Note

This is a general HR governance checklist and should not be treated as a substitute for legal advice or jurisdiction-specific compliance review.
