# HR Compliance

## Overview

HR compliance involves ensuring that people practices, employment processes, workplace documentation, and HR decisions operate within applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and organizational requirements.

This section focuses on HR governance and risk management.

---

## Core Areas

* Employment documentation
* Employee records
* Recruitment compliance
* Payroll-related HR controls
* Leave and attendance
* Workplace conduct
* Employee relations
* Health and safety coordination
* Data protection
* Policy governance
* HR audits
* Compliance risk management

---

## HR Compliance Approach

```text
Requirement
    ↓
Risk Assessment
    ↓
Current-State Review
    ↓
Gap Identification
    ↓
Corrective Action
    ↓
Documentation
    ↓
Monitoring
    ↓
Review
```

---

## HRBP Role

The HRBP helps the business:

* Identify HR risks.
* Escalate potential compliance concerns.
* Maintain appropriate documentation.
* Support consistent application of policies.
* Coordinate with legal or specialist advisers where required.
* Monitor recurring compliance issues.
* Recommend corrective actions.

---

## Important Principle

HR should not treat compliance as a checklist exercise.

The objective is to reduce avoidable risk while maintaining fair, consistent, and effective people practices.
