# HR Workforce Analytics

## Overview

This project demonstrates the use of workforce data to generate actionable HR insights for management and business leaders.

The analysis uses a fictional workforce dataset of 700 employees and examines workforce composition, turnover risk, performance, skills, critical roles, compensation and workforce requirements.

---

## Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze workforce composition
- Identify workforce trends
- Monitor employee turnover risk
- Analyze workforce performance
- Identify skills gaps
- Analyze critical-role exposure
- Examine workforce costs
- Support workforce planning decisions
- Provide management with actionable HR insights

---

## Key HR Metrics

The project will analyze:

### Workforce

- Total Headcount
- Headcount by Department
- Headcount by Location
- Employment Type
- Workforce Growth

### Talent

- Performance Distribution
- Critical Roles
- Skills Coverage
- Internal Talent Pipeline

### Retention

- Turnover Risk
- Employee Tenure
- High-Risk Population
- Retention Indicators

### Compensation

- Average Salary
- Salary by Department
- Monthly Payroll
- Annualized Payroll
- Workforce Cost

### Workforce Planning

- Required Headcount
- Current Headcount
- Workforce Gap
- Recruitment Priority

---

## HRBP Questions

The analysis is designed to help answer questions such as:

1. Where are our largest workforce gaps?
2. Which departments have the highest turnover risk?
3. Which roles are critical to business continuity?
4. Where do skills gaps exist?
5. What does our workforce cost?
6. Where should management prioritize recruitment?
7. Which employees may require development?
8. Where can internal mobility reduce external recruitment?
9. What workforce risks should leadership monitor?
10. What HR interventions should be prioritized?
