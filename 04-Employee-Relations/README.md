# Employee Relations & HR Policy

## Overview

This project demonstrates an HR Business Partner approach to employee relations, workplace policies, employee conduct, grievance management and organizational risk.

The objective is to establish fair, consistent and business-aligned approaches to managing the employment relationship.

---

## Objectives

The framework focuses on:

- Employee relations
- Grievance management
- Disciplinary processes
- Workplace investigations
- Employee conduct
- Conflict management
- Workplace communication
- Policy governance
- Manager guidance
- HR risk management
- Employee experience

---

## Core HRBP Framework

The employee-relations process follows:

**Identify → Investigate → Assess → Resolve → Document → Monitor**

---

## Key Components

### 1. Employee Relations

Managing the relationship between employees, managers and the organization.

### 2. Grievance Management

Providing a structured process for employees to raise workplace concerns.

### 3. Disciplinary Management

Ensuring misconduct and performance-related concerns are addressed through fair and consistent processes.

### 4. Workplace Investigations

Establishing facts before management decisions are made.

### 5. Conflict Resolution

Supporting constructive resolution of workplace disputes.

### 6. Policy Governance

Ensuring HR policies are communicated, understood and consistently applied.

---

## HRBP Questions

The HRBP should be able to answer:

1. What is the employee-relations issue?
2. What facts are available?
3. What policies apply?
4. Has the employee been given an opportunity to respond?
5. Has the organization applied its policies consistently?
6. What business risk exists?
7. What employee impact exists?
8. What resolution options are available?
9. What documentation is required?
10. What preventative action should management take?

## HRBP Value

Employee relations should balance:

**Employee Fairness + Business Requirements + Policy Consistency + Risk Management**

The HRBP acts as a strategic adviser to management while supporting a professional and consistent employee experience.
