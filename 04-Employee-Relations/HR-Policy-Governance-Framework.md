# HR Policy Governance Framework

## Overview

HR policy governance is the structured process through which an organization develops, approves, communicates, implements, reviews and updates its HR policies.

Effective policy governance helps ensure that HR policies remain clear, relevant, consistently applied and aligned with organizational objectives and applicable requirements.

---

# 1. Objectives

The HR policy governance framework is designed to:

- Establish clear policy ownership
- Promote consistency
- Improve policy clarity
- Support compliance
- Reduce HR risk
- Define approval responsibilities
- Improve employee understanding
- Ensure policies remain current
- Support effective implementation
- Create appropriate policy records

---

# 2. HR Policy Lifecycle

The policy lifecycle follows:

**Identify → Draft → Review → Approve → Communicate → Implement → Monitor → Review → Update**

---

# 3. Policy Identification

A new policy or policy review may be triggered by:

- Organizational change
- New business requirements
- Regulatory developments
- Employee-relations trends
- Audit findings
- Risk assessment
- Changes in technology
- Changes in workforce structure
- Repeated employee questions
- Policy gaps
- Management requests

---

# 4. Policy Development

Before developing a policy, HR should establish:

- Purpose
- Scope
- Employees covered
- Definitions
- Responsibilities
- Procedures
- Exceptions
- Approval authority
- Effective date
- Review date

---
