# Employee Engagement Survey

## 1. Purpose

This Employee Engagement Survey is designed to gather structured employee feedback on the factors that influence engagement, employee experience, workplace culture, leadership, management, communication, recognition, and career development.

The survey can be used as a practical HRBP employee-listening tool and adapted to the needs of different organizations.

---

## 2. Survey Objectives

The survey is designed to help the organization:

* Measure employee engagement
* Understand employee experience
* Identify strengths
* Identify areas requiring improvement
* Assess manager effectiveness
* Understand career development perceptions
* Evaluate internal communication
* Assess recognition practices
* Identify potential retention risks
* Generate evidence for engagement action planning

---

## 3. Confidentiality Statement

Employee responses should be treated confidentially and analyzed in aggregate wherever possible.

Individual responses should not be disclosed unnecessarily.

Where reporting group sizes are too small to protect anonymity, results should be combined with a larger group or excluded from segmented reporting.

# 16. Survey Scoring

The organization may calculate scores for individual engagement dimensions.

For example:

**Dimension Score = Total Valid Responses for Dimension ÷ Number of Valid Responses**

An overall engagement score may also be calculated using selected engagement questions.

Example:

**Overall Engagement Score = Average of Engagement Items**

Scores should be accompanied by response rates and other relevant context.

---

# 17. Favorability Analysis

Responses may also be grouped into favorable, neutral, and unfavorable categories.

| Category    | Responses                    |
| ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| Favorable   | Agree + Strongly Agree       |
| Neutral     | Neither Agree nor Disagree   |
| Unfavorable | Disagree + Strongly Disagree |

Example:

If 80 out of 100 valid respondents select either **Agree** or **Strongly Agree**, the favorable response rate is:

**80%**

# 22. Survey Frequency

The organization may use different survey approaches:

### Annual Engagement Survey

A comprehensive survey conducted once per year.

### Pulse Survey

A shorter survey conducted periodically to monitor specific issues.

### Event-Based Listening

Surveys conducted following significant employee-experience events or organizational changes.

The appropriate frequency should depend on organizational needs and the ability to act on findings.

---

# 23. Data Protection

Survey data should be:

* Collected securely
* Accessible only to authorized personnel
* Reported appropriately
* Protected from unnecessary disclosure
* Aggregated where possible
* Retained according to organizational requirements

Individual employee responses should not be used for unrelated employment decisions without an appropriate basis.
