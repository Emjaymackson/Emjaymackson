# Compensation Strategy Framework

## 1. Purpose

This framework defines how the organization designs, manages, and reviews its compensation approach to attract, retain, and motivate talent while supporting business objectives, internal equity, and external competitiveness.

The HR Business Partner (HRBP) uses this framework to guide managers, support decision-making, and ensure compensation practices remain aligned with organizational strategy and market realities.

---

## 2. Guiding Principles

Compensation decisions should be guided by the following principles:

- **Internal Equity** – Similar roles and contribution levels receive consistent treatment
- **External Competitiveness** – Pay remains competitive relative to relevant market data
- **Performance Alignment** – Differentiation based on contribution and results
- **Transparency** – Clear communication of pay philosophy and processes (within confidentiality limits)
- **Affordability** – Alignment with approved budgets and financial capacity
- **Legal Compliance** – Adherence to applicable employment laws and regulations
- **Total Rewards Perspective** – Consideration of salary, benefits, incentives, and non-financial rewards

---

## 3. Compensation Philosophy

The organization aims to:

- Position base pay competitively within the relevant market (typically around the market median, adjusted by role criticality and talent scarcity)
- Differentiate rewards based on performance, skills, and impact
- Maintain clear and consistent salary structures
- Support career progression through transparent pay ranges
- Balance fixed pay, variable pay, and benefits in a total rewards approach

---

## 4. Key Components of the Strategy

### 4.1 Salary Structure
- Job evaluation / leveling methodology
- Salary bands or grades
- Midpoints and range spreads
- Progression rules within bands

### 4.2 Market Benchmarking
- Selection of relevant peer groups and surveys
- Frequency of benchmarking (typically annual or bi-annual)
- Target market position (e.g., 50th percentile)
- Adjustments for critical or hard-to-fill roles

### 4.3 Base Pay Management
- Hiring ranges
- Promotion increases
- Merit / performance-based increases
- Equity adjustments
- Cost-of-living or general increases (where applicable)

### 4.4 Variable Pay & Incentives
- Short-term incentives / bonuses
- Performance metrics and eligibility
- Long-term incentives (if applicable)
- Recognition and spot awards

### 4.5 Benefits & Allowances
- Core benefits package
- Role- or location-specific allowances
- Flexibility and choice elements (where offered)

---

## 6. Annual Compensation Cycle (High-Level)

1. **Preparation** – Budget allocation, market data review, performance ratings finalization
2. **Guidelines** – Issuance of increase guidelines and equity parameters
3. **Manager Recommendations** – Line managers propose increases
4. **Calibration** – HRBP-facilitated reviews for consistency and fairness
5. **Approval** – Final sign-off according to authority matrix
6. **Communication** – Manager-led conversations with employees
7. **Implementation** – Payroll updates and documentation
8. **Review** – Post-cycle analysis of outcomes and lessons learned

---

## 7. HRBP Responsibilities in Compensation Strategy

- Provide managers with market insights and internal equity data
- Support salary review calibration sessions
- Flag potential pay equity or compression issues
- Advise on offer construction for critical hires
- Monitor compensation-related turnover and engagement signals
- Ensure decisions remain within policy and budget
- Support clear and respectful communication of pay decisions
- Maintain confidentiality of individual compensation data

---

## 8. Key Metrics to Monitor

- Compa-ratio distribution
- Percentage of employees within range
- Average increase percentage vs. budget
- Internal pay equity indicators (gender, level, function)
- Offer acceptance rates
- Compensation-related turnover
- Benefits utilization and cost trends
- Market positioning of key roles

---

## 9. Documentation & Confidentiality

- Individual salary information is confidential
- Only anonymized or aggregated data should be used in portfolio examples
- All real employee data must remain outside this repository
