# Total Rewards Framework

## 1. Purpose

This framework defines the organization’s approach to Total Rewards — the complete package of financial and non-financial rewards offered to employees in exchange for their contribution.

Total Rewards goes beyond base salary and benefits. It integrates compensation, benefits, recognition, development, and work experience to attract, motivate, engage, and retain talent while supporting business performance.

The HR Business Partner (HRBP) uses this framework to help managers understand the full value proposition, support retention conversations, and ensure rewards decisions are coherent and aligned with strategy.

---

## 2. Objectives

- Present a clear and compelling employee value proposition
- Align rewards with business strategy and performance
- Support attraction and retention of key talent
- Promote fairness, consistency, and transparency
- Optimize the mix of fixed pay, variable pay, benefits, and non-financial rewards
- Enable managers to have meaningful rewards conversations
- Balance employee needs with organizational affordability

---

## 3. Total Rewards Pillars

### 3.1 Compensation
- Base salary
- Salary structure and ranges
- Allowances
- Short-term incentives / bonuses
- Long-term incentives (where applicable)
- Recognition awards and spot bonuses

### 3.2 Benefits
- Health and wellness programs
- Retirement and financial security
- Leave and time-off policies
- Insurance coverage
- Flexible benefits or lifestyle support
- Employee assistance programs

### 3.3 Learning & Development
- Training and skill development
- Career pathing and progression opportunities
- Coaching and mentoring
- Educational assistance
- Leadership development programs

### 3.4 Recognition & Appreciation
- Formal recognition programs
- Informal day-to-day appreciation
- Service awards
- Performance celebration
- Peer-to-peer recognition

### 3.5 Work Experience & Environment
- Flexible work arrangements
- Inclusive and respectful culture
- Meaningful work and autonomy
- Work-life balance support
- Health, safety, and wellbeing
- Manager quality and team climate

---

## 4. Guiding Principles

- **Holistic View** – Decisions consider the full rewards package, not isolated elements
- **Strategic Alignment** – Rewards reinforce desired behaviors and business outcomes
- **Internal Equity** – Similar contribution receives similar treatment
- **External Competitiveness** – Overall package remains attractive in the market
- **Differentiation** – Higher contribution and critical skills can be rewarded differently
- **Transparency** – Employees understand how rewards work (within confidentiality limits)
- **Sustainability** – Affordable and maintainable over time
- **Employee-Centric** – Designed with understanding of what employees value

---

## 5. How Total Rewards is Applied

### Attraction
- Competitive offers that highlight the full package (not just base salary)
- Clear communication of benefits, development, and culture during recruitment

### Motivation & Performance
- Link between performance and variable pay / recognition
- Development opportunities tied to career growth
- Regular feedback and appreciation

### Retention
- Meaningful career conversations
- Recognition of contribution
- Competitive total package relative to market and internal peers
- Positive day-to-day work experience

### Manager Enablement
- Managers are equipped to explain the full value of the rewards package
- HRBP supports managers in retention and offer discussions
- Tools and talking points provided for rewards conversations

## 6. Governance

| Element                    | Primary Owner              | HRBP Role                              |
|---------------------------|----------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Overall Total Rewards strategy | HR / Compensation Leadership | Input, local adaptation, communication |
| Base pay & incentives     | Compensation team          | Guidance, equity checks, calibration   |
| Benefits design           | Benefits / HR              | Feedback, utilization insights         |
| Recognition programs      | HR / Business              | Promotion and consistency support      |
| Learning & career         | L&D / HR                   | Linking development to rewards conversations |
| Work experience           | Leaders + HR               | Culture and manager effectiveness support |

Major changes to the Total Rewards offering require appropriate leadership approval and budget confirmation.

---

## 7. Key Metrics

- Employee understanding and perceived value of the total package
- Offer acceptance rates
- Retention rates (overall and critical talent)
- Engagement / satisfaction scores related to rewards
- Utilization of key benefits and development programs
- Internal equity and market positioning indicators
- Cost of total rewards as a percentage of revenue or payroll
- Manager confidence in conducting rewards conversations

---

## 8. Communication Approach

- Explain the full package during onboarding and key career moments
- Equip managers with simple, consistent messages
- Use multiple channels (guides, sessions, one-on-ones)
- Focus on both the “what” and the “why”
- Avoid focusing only on base salary in retention or offer discussions
- Maintain confidentiality of individual compensation details

---

## 9. Confidentiality

- Individual salary and personal benefits information remains confidential
- Only aggregated or anonymized data should be used for analysis or in this portfolio
- Real employee data must never be uploaded to this repository
