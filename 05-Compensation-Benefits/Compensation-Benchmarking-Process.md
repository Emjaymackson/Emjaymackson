# Compensation Benchmarking Process

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the process for conducting compensation benchmarking. Benchmarking ensures that the organization’s pay levels remain competitive in the external market while supporting internal equity and budget discipline.

The HR Business Partner (HRBP) uses benchmarking insights to advise managers, support salary decisions, and contribute to the annual compensation review cycle.

---

## 2. Objectives

- Assess external competitiveness of current pay levels
- Inform salary structure midpoints and ranges
- Support offer decisions for new hires and promotions
- Identify roles that are significantly above or below market
- Provide data for annual salary review guidelines
- Support pay equity and compression analysis

---

## 3. Guiding Principles

- Use reliable and relevant market data sources
- Compare like-for-like roles (job matching quality is critical)
- Consider total compensation where relevant (base + variable + benefits)
- Balance market data with internal equity and affordability
- Document assumptions and data sources clearly
- Maintain strict confidentiality of individual and company data

| Phase                  | Typical Timing          | Key Activities                                      |
|------------------------|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Planning               | Q3 / early Q4           | Define scope, select surveys, confirm peer groups   |

## 5. Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Define Scope
- Decide which employee groups, levels, or functions will be included
- Confirm geographic scope (country, region, or city)
- Identify critical or hard-to-fill roles that need special attention

### Step 2: Select Data Sources
Common sources include:
- Reputable salary surveys (industry, general market, or specialized)
- Published market reports
- Participation in survey clubs or consortia
- Selective use of recruitment data or offer intelligence (with caution)

Criteria for selecting sources:
- Sample size and quality
- Industry and company-size relevance
- Recency of data
- Consistency of methodology

### Step 3: Prepare Internal Data
- Extract current base salaries, target incentives, and relevant allowances
- Ensure job titles, levels, and grades are accurate
- Clean data (remove outliers or incomplete records where justified)
- Segment by level, function, location, and other relevant factors

### Step 4: Job Matching
- Match internal roles to the closest survey benchmark jobs
- Document match quality (e.g., strong, moderate, weak)
- Avoid forcing matches where roles are genuinely unique
- Involve job experts or managers when match quality is uncertain

### Step 5: Analysis
Key comparisons typically include:
- Base salary vs. market percentiles (25th, 50th, 75th)
- Total target cash vs. market
- Compa-ratio against market midpoint
- Positioning of salary range midpoints vs. market

Look for:
- Roles significantly below market
- Roles significantly above market
- Compression between levels
- Differences by function, location, or demographic groups (for equity review)

### Step 6: Insights and Recommendations
Prepare clear findings covering:
- Overall market positioning
- Priority roles or levels requiring attention
- Recommended midpoint or range adjustments
- Implications for the coming salary review budget
- Risks related to retention or attraction

### Step 7: Application
- Feed results into salary structure updates
- Inform annual increase guidelines
- Support individual offer and retention decisions
- Track actions taken and outcomes over time

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role              | Responsibilities                                              |
|-------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Compensation / HR | Own the process, select surveys, lead analysis, present results |
| HRBP              | Provide local/contextual input, validate job matches, support managers with insights |
| Line Managers     | Clarify role content, support accurate matching, apply insights in decisions |
| Leadership        | Approve major structure or budget implications                |

---

## 7. Key Outputs

- Market positioning summary (by level / function)
- List of priority roles for adjustment or monitoring
- Recommended changes to midpoints or ranges (if any)
- Input for salary review guidelines
- Supporting data pack for calibration discussions

---

## 8. Quality Checks

Before finalizing results, verify:
- Job match quality is acceptable
- Sample sizes are sufficient
- Data is recent enough
- Internal data is clean and complete
- Outliers are understood and treated consistently
- Conclusions are supported by the data

---

## 9. Confidentiality

- Individual employee salaries must never be shared externally
- Survey data is used only under the terms of the provider
- Only aggregated or anonymized insights appear in this portfolio
- Real company or employee data must not be uploaded here
