# Learning Needs Analysis Framework

## 1. Purpose

This framework provides a structured approach for identifying, prioritizing, and addressing learning and development needs across the organization. A robust Learning Needs Analysis (LNA) ensures that training and development investments are aligned with business priorities, close real capability gaps, and deliver measurable value.

The HR Business Partner (HRBP) plays a key role in connecting business challenges to capability requirements and supporting managers in translating those needs into practical development actions.

---

## 2. Objectives

- Identify current and future skill and capability gaps
- Align learning investments with business strategy and priorities
- Support individual, team, and organizational development planning
- Improve the relevance and impact of learning interventions
- Enable better prioritization of limited L&D resources
- Strengthen the link between development, performance, and succession

---

## 3. Levels of Learning Needs Analysis

| Level              | Focus                                      | Typical Methods                          |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Organizational     | Strategic capabilities and future needs    | Business strategy review, leadership input, workforce planning |
| Team / Functional  | Role and team-specific skill gaps          | Manager discussions, team performance data, process reviews |
| Individual         | Personal development and career growth     | Performance reviews, IDPs, career conversations, self-assessment |

---

## 4. Guiding Principles

- Start with business outcomes, not courses
- Focus on capabilities that drive performance
- Involve managers and employees in the process
- Prioritize based on impact and urgency
- Distinguish between skill gaps, motivation issues, and systemic barriers
- Use multiple data sources for a balanced view
- Keep the process practical and actionable

---

## 5. Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Clarify Business Context
- Understand current business priorities and challenges
- Identify critical roles and future capability requirements
- Review strategic plans, workforce plans, and performance trends

### Step 2: Gather Data
Sources may include:
- Performance data and appraisal outcomes
- Manager and employee feedback
- Skills assessments or competency reviews
- Exit interviews and retention data
- Customer or operational feedback
- Observation of work processes
- Existing learning participation and effectiveness data

### Step 3: Identify Gaps
- Compare current capabilities against required standards
- Distinguish knowledge, skill, and behavior gaps
- Note differences by level, function, or location
- Highlight critical gaps that affect business results

### Step 4: Prioritize Needs
Prioritization criteria typically include:
- Impact on business performance or risk
- Number of people affected
- Urgency / time sensitivity
- Feasibility and cost of closing the gap
- Alignment with strategic priorities

### Step 5: Recommend Solutions
Possible responses:
- Formal training or e-learning
- On-the-job learning and stretch assignments
- Coaching or mentoring
- Process or tool improvements (non-training solutions)
- Role redesign or additional resources
- Leadership or cultural interventions

### Step 6: Agree Actions and Owners
- Confirm priorities with business leaders
- Assign clear owners and timelines
- Link to individual development plans where relevant
- Define how success will be measured

### Step 7: Review and Refine
- Track implementation and early results
- Gather feedback on relevance and application
- Adjust future analysis cycles based on lessons learned

---

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role              | Responsibilities                                              |
|-------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Business Leaders  | Define priorities, validate critical capabilities, sponsor actions |
| Managers          | Identify team and individual gaps, support development actions |
| HRBP              | Facilitate the process, connect business needs to L&D, support prioritization |
| L&D / Talent Team | Design solutions, provide expertise, manage programs          |
| Employees         | Participate in assessments, own their development             |

---

## 7. Key Outputs

- Prioritized list of learning and capability needs
- Recommended development interventions
- Input for the L&D plan and budget
- Guidance for individual development plans
- Clear success measures for key initiatives

---

## 8. Key Metrics

- Percentage of critical capability gaps addressed
- Manager and employee satisfaction with development relevance
- Application of learning on the job
- Improvement in targeted performance indicators
- Internal mobility and readiness for key roles
- Learning investment aligned to priority needs

---

## 9. Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- Jumping straight to training without diagnosing the real need
- Relying only on employee wish lists
- Ignoring non-training solutions
- Failing to prioritize
- Not involving managers in the process
- Measuring only attendance instead of impact

---

## 10. Confidentiality

- Individual performance and development data must remain confidential
- Only aggregated or anonymized insights should be used in this portfolio
- Real employee information must never be uploaded
