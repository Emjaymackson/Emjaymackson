# Learning Curriculum Framework

## 1. Purpose

The Learning Curriculum Framework provides a structured approach for designing and organizing learning programs across different employee populations, job families, career levels, and capability requirements.

The framework ensures that learning is intentional, progressive, business-aligned, and connected to the organization's Capability Framework and Learning & Development Strategy.

---

## 2. Objectives

The curriculum framework aims to:

- Organize learning into clear development pathways
- Align learning content with business and capability requirements
- Provide appropriate learning for different career levels
- Support technical, functional, behavioral, and leadership development
- Create consistent learning standards across the organization
- Support career progression and internal mobility
- Reduce duplication of learning programs
- Enable measurement of learning outcomes

---

## 3. Curriculum Architecture

The learning curriculum should generally consist of five layers:

1. **Core Learning**
2. **Functional Learning**
3. **Leadership Learning**
4. **Career Development**
5. **Future Skills**

Each layer should contain learning programs appropriate to the employee population and required capability level.

---

## 4. Core Learning Curriculum

Core learning consists of capabilities expected across the organization.

Potential topics include:

- Organizational values
- Code of conduct
- Communication
- Teamwork
- Customer focus
- Problem-solving
- Professionalism
- Workplace ethics
- Data protection awareness
- Information security awareness
- Health and safety
- Diversity and respectful workplace practices

Core learning may be mandatory or recommended depending on the subject.

---

### Finance

- Financial reporting
- Budget management
- Financial analysis

- Risk management

### Sales

- Prospecting
- Consultative selling
- Negotiation
- Account management

- Quality management

## 7. Career Development Curriculum

Career development learning should support employees as they move through different career stages.

Potential programs include:

- Personal development
- Interview skills
- Professional communication

## 8. Future Skills Curriculum

The organization should periodically identify emerging capabilities that may affect future workforce requirements.

Potential areas include:

- Artificial intelligence

- Automation
- Advanced analytics
- Cybersecurity awareness
- Digital collaboration
- Innovation
- Change agility

Future-skills programs should be reviewed as technology and business requirements evolve.

---

## 9. Learning Pathways

A learning pathway should show how employees can progress from foundational knowledge to advanced capability.

Example:

**Foundation → Developing → Proficient → Advanced → Expert**

For a management pathway:

**Individual Contributor → Emerging Leader → First-Line Manager → Senior Manager → Executive Leader**

Each stage should have defined:

- Capabilities
- Learning requirements
- Experience requirements
- Development activities
- Assessment criteria

## 11. Curriculum Design Process

### Step 1: Identify Business Requirements

Understand current and future organizational priorities.

### Step 2: Identify Capability Requirements

Determine the capabilities required to deliver those priorities.

### Step 3: Identify Learning Gaps

Use Training Needs Analysis and capability assessments.

### Step 4: Define Learning Objectives

Specify what participants should know, understand, or demonstrate.

### Step 5: Structure the Curriculum

Group learning into logical pathways and modules.

### Step 6: Select Delivery Methods

Choose appropriate methods such as:

- Classroom training
- Virtual training
- E-learning
- Workshops
- Coaching
- Mentoring
- Job assignments
- Job rotations

### Step 7: Define Assessment

Determine how learning and capability will be evaluated.

### Step 8: Implement

Launch the curriculum according to agreed priorities and schedules.

### Step 9: Evaluate

Review participant feedback, learning outcomes, application, and business results.

### Step 10: Refresh

Update the curriculum as business and capability requirements change.

## 12. Learning Module Design

Each curriculum module should contain:

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Module Title | Name of the learning module |
| Target Audience | Employees expected to participate |
| Capability | Capability being developed |
| Learning Objective | Intended learning outcome |
| Content | Topics covered |
| Delivery Method | How learning will be delivered |
| Duration | Expected learning time |
| Assessment | How learning will be measured |
| Application | How learning will be used at work |
| Success Measure | Expected outcome |

### Digital Learning

- Webinars

- Digital resources

### Experiential Learning

- Stretch assignments
- Projects

---

## 14. Mandatory vs Developmental Learning

Learning should be categorized appropriately.

### Mandatory Learning

Required because of:

- Legal requirements
- Regulatory obligations
- Health and safety
- Information security
- Data protection
- Organizational policies

### Developmental Learning

Designed to improve capability and career growth.

Examples include:

- Leadership programs
- Technical training
- Professional certifications
- Career development
- Advanced functional skills

Separating these categories helps management understand compliance requirements versus discretionary development investment.

## 16. Curriculum Review

The curriculum should be reviewed periodically.

Review inputs may include:

- Business strategy
- Capability assessments
- Training Needs Analysis
- Employee feedback
- Manager feedback
- Learning outcomes
- Performance data
- Technology changes
- Industry developments
- Regulatory changes

Outdated or ineffective modules should be revised, replaced, or discontinued.

---

## 17. Learning Quality Standards

Learning programs should be:

- Relevant
- Accurate
- Practical
- Accessible
- Appropriate for the target audience
- Consistent with organizational policies
- Aligned with defined learning objectives
- Supported by appropriate learning materials

Where external providers are used, provider quality should be assessed before engagement.

---

## 18. Curriculum Measurement

Key measures may include:

- Enrollment rate
- Completion rate
- Assessment results
- Participant satisfaction
- Learning application
- Capability improvement
- Internal mobility
- Promotion readiness
- Leadership pipeline development
- Performance improvement
- Business outcomes linked to learning

---

## 19. Curriculum and Career Progression

Learning pathways should support career development by showing employees what capabilities are required for progression.

Example:

| Career Stage | Capability Focus | Development Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | Foundational | Training + Coaching |
| Professional | Functional | Training + Projects |
| Senior | Advanced | Projects + Mentoring |
| Manager | Leadership | Leadership Program + Coaching |
| Senior Manager | Strategic | Executive Development + Strategic Projects |

Progression should not depend solely on completing training. Demonstrated capability, performance, experience, and role requirements should also be considered.

---

## 20. Curriculum and Succession Planning

Learning curricula should support the development of successors for critical roles.

Development may include:

- Leadership programs
- Cross-functional assignments
- Strategic projects
- Acting responsibilities
- Executive mentoring
- Technical specialization
- Business exposure

Learning records should be used as one source of evidence and should not replace broader succession assessments.

---

## 21. HRBP Responsibilities

The HRBP should:

- Connect curriculum priorities to business strategy
- Identify capability requirements with business leaders
- Support curriculum design
- Facilitate Training Needs Analysis
- Advise on learning priorities
- Connect learning with career and succession planning
- Monitor learning outcomes
- Identify capability trends
- Support leadership development
- Evaluate learning investment and business impact

---

## 22. Confidentiality & Data Protection

Learning records should be handled securely.

The organization should:

- Restrict access to authorized users
- Protect employee learning records
- Avoid publishing identifiable employee information
- Use anonymized information for portfolio examples
- Keep confidential assessment and performance data outside public repositories
