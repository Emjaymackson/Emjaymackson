# Learning & Development

## Overview

Learning & Development (L&D) focuses on building the skills, knowledge, and capabilities employees need to perform effectively today and prepare for future roles.

The HRBP supports the business by identifying capability gaps, enabling targeted development, supporting career growth, and helping create a learning culture that drives performance and retention.

---

## Key Areas

- Learning needs analysis
- Capability and skills frameworks
- Training program design and evaluation
- Leadership development
- Career development and pathing
- Coaching and mentoring
- On-the-job learning and stretch assignments
- Learning culture and continuous development
- Training effectiveness measurement
- Talent readiness and succession support
- Individual development plans (IDPs)
- Digital and self-directed learning

---

## HRBP Responsibilities

The HRBP supports Learning & Development by:

- Identifying skill and capability gaps with business leaders
- Translating business priorities into learning needs
- Supporting the creation and prioritization of development plans
- Advising managers on coaching and career conversations
- Linking development to performance and succession
- Monitoring learning participation and effectiveness
- Supporting leadership development initiatives
- Encouraging a culture of continuous learning
- Ensuring development opportunities are fair and accessible

---

## Key Learning & Development Metrics

- Training hours per employee
- Learning participation / completion rates
- Training effectiveness / application scores
- Capability gap closure
- Internal mobility / promotion rates
- Leadership bench strength
- Employee perception of development opportunities
- Time-to-competency for critical roles
- Return on learning investment (where measurable)
- Manager effectiveness in developing others

---

## Core L&D Approaches

Effective development typically combines:

- Formal training (workshops, courses, e-learning)
- On-the-job learning (projects, stretch roles, job rotation)
- Coaching and mentoring
- Feedback and reflection
- Self-directed learning
- Action learning and peer learning
- Leadership and high-potential programs

---

## Governance

Learning & Development activities should be guided by:

- Business strategy and capability requirements
- Organizational values and culture
- Budget and resource availability
- Fair access and inclusion principles
- Quality and relevance of learning content
- Evaluation of impact on performance and behavior

---

## Confidentiality

No confidential employee performance data, development plans linked to identifiable individuals, or sensitive talent information should be uploaded to this portfolio.

All examples and datasets must be fictionalized or fully anonymized.
