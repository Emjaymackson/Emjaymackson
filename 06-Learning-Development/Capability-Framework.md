# Capability Framework

## 1. Purpose

This framework defines the key capabilities required for employees to perform effectively and contribute to organizational success. It provides a common language for discussing skills, behaviors, and knowledge across roles and levels.

The HR Business Partner (HRBP) uses the capability framework to support development planning, performance conversations, succession discussions, and the identification of learning needs.

---

## 2. Objectives

- Create clarity on what good performance looks like
- Support consistent assessment of current capabilities
- Guide individual and team development planning
- Inform learning priorities and program design
- Strengthen succession and talent discussions
- Enable fairer and more transparent career conversations
- Align people development with business strategy

---

## 3. Structure of the Framework

A practical capability framework typically includes:

### Core / Organizational Capabilities
Capabilities expected of most or all employees (e.g., collaboration, customer focus, accountability, adaptability).

### Leadership Capabilities
Capabilities required of people managers and leaders (e.g., leading people, developing others, strategic thinking, driving results).

### Functional / Technical Capabilities
Role- or function-specific knowledge and skills (e.g., financial acumen, project management, technical expertise).

### Proficiency Levels
Clear descriptions of what each capability looks like at different levels of mastery (e.g., Foundational, Proficient, Advanced, Expert).

---

## 4. Guiding Principles

- Keep the framework simple and usable
- Focus on capabilities that truly differentiate performance
- Make behavioral indicators observable and specific
- Align with organizational values and strategy
- Design for practical application in conversations and decisions
- Review and update periodically as business needs evolve
- Ensure accessibility and fairness across employee groups

---

## 6. Developing or Refreshing the Framework

### Step 1: Define Scope
- Decide which employee groups and levels the framework will cover
- Confirm whether to include core, leadership, and functional capabilities

### Step 2: Gather Input
- Review business strategy and critical success factors
- Consult leaders, high performers, and subject-matter experts
- Analyze existing performance and competency data
- Consider external benchmarks where useful

### Step 3: Draft Capabilities and Indicators
- Define each capability clearly
- Write observable behavioral indicators at each proficiency level
- Keep language simple and free of jargon

### Step 4: Validate
- Test with managers and employees for clarity and relevance
- Check for overlap, gaps, or bias
- Refine based on feedback

### Step 5: Implement
- Integrate into performance, development, and talent processes
- Train managers on how to use the framework
- Provide supporting tools and conversation guides

### Step 6: Maintain
- Review periodically (typically every 2–3 years or after major strategy shifts)
- Update as roles and business priorities change

| Role              | Responsibilities                                              |
|-------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Leadership        | Sponsor the framework and model the capabilities              |
| HR / Talent / L&D | Design, maintain, and support application of the framework    |
| HRBP              | Help managers apply it in real conversations and decisions    |
| Managers          | Use the framework in performance, development, and career discussions |
| Employees         | Understand expectations and take ownership of development     |

---

## 8. Key Success Factors

- Manager confidence and skill in using the framework
- Clear link to real business outcomes
- Integration into existing people processes (not a standalone tool)
- Regular reinforcement and communication
- Avoiding over-complexity

---

## 9. Confidentiality

- Individual capability assessments are confidential
- Only aggregated or anonymized information should appear in this portfolio
- Real employee data must never be uploaded
