# Training Plan Template

## 1. Purpose

This template provides a structured method for converting identified learning and capability needs into an actionable training plan.

It helps HR, managers, and business leaders define what employees need to learn, why the learning is required, who should participate, how it will be delivered, and how effectiveness will be measured.

## 3. Business Need

### Business Objective

Describe the organizational or departmental objective the training is intended to support.

### Current Situation

Describe the current capability, performance, process, or knowledge issue.

### Identified Gap

Clearly state the gap between the required capability and current capability.

### Business Impact

Explain the potential impact of the gap on:

- Productivity
- Quality
- Customer experience
- Revenue
- Employee performance
- Compliance
- Operational efficiency
- Employee retention
- Future workforce capability

---

## 4. Learning Objectives

Learning objectives should describe what participants should be able to demonstrate after completing the program.

Objectives should be specific and measurable.

Example:

By the end of the program, participants should be able to:

1. Explain the organization's performance-management process.
2. Set measurable employee objectives.
3. Conduct effective performance conversations.
4. Provide constructive performance feedback.
5. Document performance issues appropriately.

## 7. Delivery Method

The organization may use one or more delivery methods.

### Instructor-Led Training

Suitable for:

- Complex concepts
- Practical workshops
- Group discussions
- Leadership development

### Virtual Training

Suitable for:

- Distributed teams
- Knowledge-based learning
- Short learning sessions
- Cross-location programs

### E-Learning

Suitable for:

- Self-paced learning
- Mandatory training
- Knowledge development
- Scalable programs

### On-the-Job Learning

Suitable for:

- Technical skills
- Operational processes
- Role-specific capability
- Practical application

### Coaching and Mentoring

Suitable for:

- Leadership development
- Behavioral development
- Career development
- Performance improvement

### Blended Learning

Combines multiple methods to support learning before, during, and after formal training.

## 8. Training Provider

### Internal Provider

Where appropriate, training may be delivered by:

- HR / L&D
- Subject-matter experts
- Managers
- Internal facilitators
- Experienced employees

### External Provider

External providers may be considered when:

- Specialized expertise is required
- Professional certification is involved
- Internal capability is unavailable
- Independent assessment is required

Provider selection should consider:

- Relevant expertise
- Track record
- Program quality
- Cost
- Participant requirements
- Delivery method
- Evaluation approach

## 11. Participant Preparation

Participants may be required to complete activities before training.

Examples include:

- Pre-training assessment
- Reading materials
- Self-assessment
- Manager discussion
- Relevant business case
- Pre-training survey

Preparation should be proportionate to the program and its objectives.

---

## 12. Learning Assessment

Learning may be assessed using:

- Knowledge tests
- Practical exercises
- Simulations
- Case studies
- Demonstrations
- Role plays
- Skills assessments
- Certification examinations

Assessment methods should reflect the learning objectives.

---

## 13. Post-Training Application

Managers should support participants in applying what they have learned.

Possible reinforcement activities include:

- Practical assignments
- Coaching sessions
- Follow-up discussions
- Job aids
- Refresher sessions
- Peer learning
- Stretch assignments
- Manager observation

---

## 14. Training Evaluation

Training effectiveness should be evaluated at multiple levels.

### Level 1: Participant Reaction

Measure:

- Satisfaction
- Relevance
- Quality of delivery
- Facilitator effectiveness

### Level 2: Learning

Measure:

- Knowledge gained
- Skills acquired
- Assessment results

### Level 3: Application

Measure whether employees apply the learning in their roles.

### Level 4: Business Results

Where measurable, evaluate changes in relevant business outcomes.

Examples:

- Productivity
- Quality
- Customer satisfaction
- Sales performance
- Error reduction
- Employee performance
- Compliance

## 16. Roles and Responsibilities

### HRBP / L&D

- Coordinate the training plan
- Validate business need
- Support needs analysis
- Recommend appropriate interventions
- Monitor participation
- Coordinate evaluation
- Report outcomes

### Manager

- Identify employee development needs
- Nominate appropriate participants
- Support attendance
- Reinforce learning
- Monitor workplace application
- Provide feedback

### Employee

- Participate actively
- Complete required preparation
- Apply learning
- Complete assessments
- Participate in follow-up activities

### Training Provider

- Deliver agreed content
- Provide learning materials
- Assess learning where applicable
- Provide participant feedback
- Support evaluation requirements

---

## 17. Training Risk Management

Potential risks should be identified before implementation.

| Risk | Potential Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Low attendance | Reduced learning impact | Manager confirmation and scheduling |
| Poor content relevance | Low application | Validate content against TNA |
| Weak manager support | Limited transfer of learning | Manager reinforcement plan |
| Inadequate provider quality | Poor learning outcomes | Provider evaluation |
| Budget constraints | Reduced scope | Prioritize critical needs |
| Operational workload | Participants unable to attend | Advance scheduling |

---

## 18. Training Completion

After the program, record:

- Attendance
- Completion status
- Assessment results
- Certification status where applicable
- Participant feedback
- Manager feedback
- Follow-up actions
- Business outcomes

---

## 19. Training Plan Review

The training plan should be reviewed periodically to determine:

- Whether objectives remain relevant
- Whether participants are applying the learning
- Whether additional support is required
- Whether the program should be repeated
- Whether content should be updated
- Whether the program produced measurable value

---

## 20. Confidentiality & Documentation

Training records should be handled securely.

The organization should:

- Protect employee learning records
- Restrict access to authorized personnel
- Maintain accurate attendance and completion records
- Use anonymized data for portfolio examples
- Keep confidential employee information outside public repositories
