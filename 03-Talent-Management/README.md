# Talent Management & Succession Planning

## Overview

This project demonstrates an HR Business Partner approach to identifying, developing and retaining organizational talent.

The project focuses on talent reviews, succession planning, critical-role management, employee development and leadership pipeline development.

---

## Objectives

The objectives are to:

- Identify high-potential employees
- Identify high-performing employees
- Assess talent against performance and potential
- Identify critical roles
- Assess succession risks
- Develop successor pipelines
- Create individual development plans
- Support internal mobility
- Strengthen leadership capability
- Reduce key-person dependency

---

## Core HRBP Framework

The talent-management process follows:

**Identify → Assess → Develop → Deploy → Retain → Review**

---

## Key Components

### 1. Talent Review

A structured process for reviewing employee performance, potential and career readiness.

### 2. 9-Box Talent Matrix

Employees are assessed using:

- Performance
- Potential

The matrix helps management identify different talent populations and appropriate development actions.

### 3. Succession Planning

Critical roles are assessed based on:

- Business impact
- Vacancy risk
- Successor availability
- Successor readiness
- Capability gaps

### 4. Individual Development Plans

Employees receive structured development plans based on:

- Current capability
- Career aspirations
- Performance
- Future-role requirements
- Skills gaps

---

## HRBP Questions

The analysis is designed to answer:

1. Who are our high performers?
2. Who demonstrates high potential?
3. Which roles are business-critical?
4. Which critical roles have no successor?
5. Which successors are ready now?
6. Which employees could be ready within 1–2 years?
7. What capability gaps exist?
8. Where should development investment be directed?
9. Which employees are at risk of leaving?
10. How strong is our leadership pipeline?

---

## Expected Outputs

This project will contain:

- Talent Review Framework

- Succession Planning Framework
- Critical Role Analysis
- Individual Development Plan
- Talent Review Template
- HRBP Talent Recommendations

---

## HRBP Value

Talent management should connect people capability with business strategy.

The HRBP's role is to ensure that the organization has:

**The right people → in the right roles → with the right capabilities → at the right time.**
