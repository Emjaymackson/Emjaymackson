# Workforce Planning

## Overview

Workforce planning is a strategic HR process used to ensure that an organization has the right number of people, with the right capabilities, in the right roles, at the right time.

This project demonstrates a structured approach to workforce planning from an HR Business Partner perspective.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

- Assess current workforce capacity
- Identify workforce gaps
- Determine future talent requirements
- Align workforce needs with business objectives
- Identify critical roles and capabilities
- Support recruitment and talent-development decisions
- Improve workforce productivity and utilization
- Support succession and continuity planning

---

## 🔎 Workforce Planning Process

### 1. Understand the Business Strategy

The HRBP first develops an understanding of:

- Business objectives
- Growth plans
- New business units
- Revenue targets
- Operational changes
- Technology requirements
- Organizational restructuring

### 2. Analyze the Current Workforce

Key workforce information includes:

- Current headcount
- Department
- Job role
- Employee tenure
- Skills and competencies
- Performance
- Employment status
- Critical positions
- Retirement/attrition risks

### 3. Identify Workforce Gaps

Workforce gaps may include:

- Headcount shortages
- Skills shortages
- Leadership gaps
- Critical-role dependency
- Succession gaps
- Productivity gaps

### 4. Forecast Future Workforce Requirements

Future workforce requirements are assessed based on:

- Business growth
- Expansion
- New projects
- Technology
- Operational requirements
- Expected employee turnover
- New capabilities

### 5. Develop Workforce Actions

Possible interventions include:

- Recruitment
- Internal mobility
- Reskilling
- Upskilling
- Succession planning
- Outsourcing
- Workforce restructuring
- Leadership development

| Metric | Purpose |

| Turnover Rate | Measures employee exits |

| Time-to-Fill | Measures recruitment efficiency |

## 🧮 Workforce Gap Analysis

A basic workforce gap can be calculated as:

**Required Workforce − Current Workforce = Workforce Gap**

Example:

| Department | Current | Required | Gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Sales | 18 | 25 | 7 |
| Operations | 30 | 35 | 5 |
| Finance | 8 | 10 | 2 |
| HR | 5 | 6 | 1 |

The HRBP can then work with business leaders to determine how each gap should be addressed.

---

## 🧠 HRBP Perspective

Workforce planning should not operate as a purely administrative HR exercise.

The HRBP should work with business leadership to understand:

**Where is the business going?**

↓

**What capabilities will the business need?**

↓

**What workforce do we currently have?**

↓

**Where are the gaps?**

↓

**How do we close those gaps?**

↓

**How do we measure the result?**
