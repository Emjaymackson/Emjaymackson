# Organizational Development Framework

## Purpose

This framework provides a structured method for diagnosing and improving organizational effectiveness.

---

## 1. Diagnose

Assess:

* Business strategy
* Organizational structure
* Workforce capability
* Leadership
* Culture
* Processes
* Technology
* Employee experience

---

## 2. Identify Organizational Gaps

Examples:

* Unclear responsibilities
* Duplicate roles
* Leadership gaps
* Poor communication
* Skills shortages
* Inefficient processes
* Weak accountability
* Poor coordination between departments

---

## 3. Intervention Design

Possible interventions include:

* Role redesign
* Restructuring
* Leadership development
* Team development
* Process improvement
* Capability development
* Policy changes
* Communication programmes

---

## 4. Stakeholder Alignment

Stakeholders may include:

* Executive leadership
* Department heads
* Managers
* HR
* Employees
* Project teams

---

## 5. Implementation

Each OD initiative should define:

* Objective
* Scope
* Stakeholders
* Timeline
* Responsibilities
* Resources
* Risks
* Communication
* Success measures

---

## 6. Measurement

Possible measures:

* Productivity
* Employee engagement
* Turnover
* Internal mobility
* Role clarity
* Customer outcomes
* Process efficiency
* Leadership effectiveness

---

## 7. HRBP Principle

OD should solve an identifiable organizational problem.

The intervention should not exist simply because it is an HR initiative.
