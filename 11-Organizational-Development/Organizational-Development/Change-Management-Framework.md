# Change Management Framework

## Purpose

Change management provides a structured approach for helping employees and leaders transition from a current state to a desired future state.

---

## Change Lifecycle

```text
Current State
     ↓
Change Case
     ↓
Stakeholder Analysis
     ↓
Change Design
     ↓
Communication
     ↓
Capability Support
     ↓
Implementation
     ↓
Adoption
     ↓
Measurement
```

---

## 1. Define the Change

Document:

* What is changing?
* Why is it changing?
* Who is affected?
* When will it happen?
* What will success look like?

---

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis

Consider:

* Influence
* Impact
* Support level
* Concerns
* Information needs

---

## 3. Communication Plan

| Audience   | Message                 | Channel           | Timing        |
| ---------- | ----------------------- | ----------------- | ------------- |
| Leadership | Business rationale      | Meeting           | Before launch |
| Managers   | Implementation details  | Workshop          | Before launch |
| Employees  | Impact and expectations | Town hall/email   | Launch        |
| HR         | Support process         | Internal briefing | Throughout    |

---

## 4. Employee Readiness

Assess:

* Awareness
* Understanding
* Capability
* Confidence
* Adoption

---

## 5. Resistance

Resistance should be investigated rather than automatically treated as misconduct.

Potential causes:

* Poor communication
* Lack of trust
* Insufficient training
* Workload concerns
* Unclear expectations
* Previous failed changes

---

## 6. Change Risks

Examples:

* Poor communication
* Leadership inconsistency
* Insufficient training
* Employee disengagement
* Operational disruption

---

## 7. Measurement

Measure:

* Adoption
* Training completion
* Employee feedback
* Operational performance
* Productivity
* Engagement
* Change-related issues

---

## HRBP Role

The HRBP supports:

* Leadership alignment
* Workforce impact assessment
* Communication
* Manager readiness
* Employee relations
* Capability building
* Adoption measurement
