# Organizational Design Framework

## Purpose

Organizational design determines how work, responsibilities, authority, reporting relationships, and resources are structured to support business objectives.

---

## 1. Business Strategy

Start with:

* Business objectives
* Growth plans
* Customer requirements
* Geographic expansion
* Technology requirements
* Revenue model

---

## 2. Organizational Structure

Common structures include:

* Functional
* Divisional
* Geographic
* Matrix
* Product-based
* Project-based

The appropriate structure depends on organizational requirements.

---

## 3. Role Clarity

Every role should have:

* Job title
* Purpose
* Key responsibilities
* Reporting line
* Decision authority
* Required capability
* Performance expectations

---

## 4. Span of Control

Review:

* Number of direct reports
* Manager workload
* Leadership capacity
* Decision speed
* Manager capability

---

## 5. Accountability

Use tools such as a RACI framework:

| Role        | Responsibility       |
| ----------- | -------------------- |
| Responsible | Performs the work    |
| Accountable | Owns the outcome     |
| Consulted   | Provides input       |
| Informed    | Receives information |

---

## 6. Design Review Questions

* Are roles clearly defined?
* Are there duplicated responsibilities?
* Are decisions made at the appropriate level?
* Are reporting lines logical?
* Are managers overloaded?
* Are critical capabilities available?

---

## 7. HRBP Role

The HRBP helps leadership connect organizational design with:

* Workforce planning
* Recruitment
* Performance
* Compensation
* Capability
* Employee experience

---

## 8. Success Measures

Possible indicators:

* Faster decision-making
* Reduced duplication
* Improved accountability
* Better manager capacity
* Improved productivity
* Stronger employee clarity
