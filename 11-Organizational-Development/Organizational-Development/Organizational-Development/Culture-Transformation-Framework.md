# Culture Transformation Framework

## Purpose

Culture transformation involves intentionally strengthening the behaviours, leadership practices, systems, and employee experiences that support organizational objectives.

---

## Culture Model

```text
Business Strategy
       ↓
Desired Behaviours
       ↓
Leadership Practices
       ↓
HR Systems
       ↓
Employee Experience
       ↓
Observed Culture
```

---

## 1. Diagnose Current Culture

Sources may include:

* Employee surveys
* Interviews
* Focus groups
* Exit interviews
* Engagement data
* Employee relations cases
* Leadership feedback

---

## 2. Define Desired Culture

Identify:

* Core values
* Expected behaviours
* Leadership standards
* Communication practices
* Accountability expectations

---

## 3. Align HR Systems

Culture should be reinforced through:

* Recruitment
* Onboarding
* Performance management
* Recognition
* Promotion
* Learning
* Leadership development
* Employee relations

---

## 4. Leadership Role

Leaders should demonstrate the behaviours expected from employees.

Culture transformation cannot depend entirely on employee training.

---

## 5. Culture Action Plan

| Area          | Action                 | Owner      | Measure       |
| ------------- | ---------------------- | ---------- | ------------- |
| Leadership    | Manager workshops      | HR         | Completion    |
| Communication | Employee forums        | HR/Leaders | Participation |
| Recognition   | Recognition programme  | HR         | Usage         |
| Performance   | Behavioural objectives | Managers   | Adoption      |

---

## 6. Measurement

Possible indicators:

* Engagement
* Trust
* Leadership scores
* Employee relations cases
* Voluntary turnover
* Absence
* Recognition participation
* Internal mobility

---

## Portfolio Application

The framework demonstrates the ability to connect culture with practical HR systems and organizational outcomes.
