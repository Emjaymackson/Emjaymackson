# Organizational Development

## Overview

Organizational Development (OD) focuses on improving organizational effectiveness through structured changes to people, processes, structures, leadership, culture, and ways of working.

For an HR Business Partner, OD connects people strategy with business strategy.

---

## Core OD Areas

* Organizational design
* Workforce structure
* Role clarity
* Change management
* Leadership effectiveness
* Culture transformation
* Team effectiveness
* Capability development
* Succession planning
* Employee experience

---

## OD Approach

```text
Business Need
     ↓
Organizational Diagnosis
     ↓
Identify Gaps
     ↓
Design Intervention
     ↓
Stakeholder Alignment
     ↓
Implementation
     ↓
Measure Impact
     ↓
Continuous Improvement
```

---

## HRBP Role

The HRBP may:

* Diagnose organizational issues.
* Review structures and roles.
* Advise leaders.
* Facilitate organizational changes.
* Support managers through change.
* Assess workforce capability.
* Identify culture gaps.
* Monitor employee impact.
* Measure intervention effectiveness.
