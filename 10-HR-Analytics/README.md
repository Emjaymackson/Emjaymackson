# HR Analytics

## Overview

HR Analytics is the process of using workforce data to understand people-related trends, identify business risks, support decision-making, and measure the effectiveness of HR initiatives.

For an HR Business Partner, analytics is not simply about producing reports. The objective is to translate workforce information into meaningful business insight.

This section demonstrates an HRBP approach to:

* Workforce analytics
* Headcount analysis
* Recruitment analytics
* Turnover and retention
* Absence analytics
* Performance analytics
* Compensation analysis
* Learning and development analytics
* Employee engagement analytics
* Employee relations analytics
* Workforce planning
* HR dashboards
* Data-driven decision-making

---

## Purpose

The purpose of this section is to demonstrate the ability to:

1. Define relevant HR metrics.
2. Collect and structure workforce data.
3. Identify workforce trends.
4. Analyse HR problems using evidence.
5. Translate data into business insights.
6. Recommend practical actions.
7. Measure the impact of HR interventions.
8. Present workforce information to management clearly.

## HRBP Role in Analytics

The HRBP should connect people data to business decisions.

Key responsibilities include:

* Defining the business question.
* Selecting relevant metrics.
* Interpreting workforce data.
* Identifying trends and patterns.
* Challenging assumptions.
* Identifying potential root causes.
* Presenting insights to management.
* Recommending interventions.
* Monitoring outcomes.

---

## Data Quality Principles

HR analytics should be based on:

* Accurate data
* Complete records
* Consistent definitions
* Reliable reporting periods
* Appropriate segmentation
* Documented assumptions
* Controlled access
* Confidential handling of employee information

Poor data quality can produce misleading conclusions even when the analysis itself is technically correct.
